# DAJING-Eighteen-Months-of-the-Throne


<h3>The Old Emperor is Dead. You Take the Chair.</h3>
<p>
  <strong>DAJING: Eighteen Months of the Throne</strong> is a dark, text-driven political survival and resource-management game inspired by late-imperial palace intrigue and unofficial history.
</p>
<p>
  Step onto a throne surrounded by wolves: a regent Empress Dowager pulling strings behind the curtain, a calculating Grand Secretary, a starving army at the northern frontier, and an empty treasury. Across three escalating acts, every memorial you stamp with the imperial seal shifts the fragile balance of power.
</p>

<h4>⚖️ Four Pillars of Survival</h4>
<ul>
  <li><strong>Imperial Health (龙体):</strong> Beware of chronic poison in your evening tonic and the toll of sleepless nights.</li>
  <li><strong>Sanity (心力):</strong> High-stakes court paranoia will break your mind long before an assassin's blade reaches you.</li>
  <li><strong>Treasury (国库):</strong> Fund the border garrisons, relieve winter blizzards, or squeeze corrupt officials dry.</li>
  <li><strong>Court Favor (朝望):</strong> Keep the ministers, eunuchs, and generals in check—or be reduced to a helpless puppet.</li>
</ul>

<h4>✨ Key Features</h4>
<ul>
  <li><strong>Three-Act Political Escalation:</strong> Navigate from the tense Regency (Act I) through brutal Factional Strife (Act II) to the final Reckoning (Act III).</li>
  <li><strong>Meaningful Consequences:</strong> Branching event flags, perilous palace factions, and <strong>9 distinct endings</strong>—from a glorious Golden Age revival to quiet abdication or complete madness.</li>
  <li><strong>Bilingual Support:</strong> Full English and Simplified Chinese (简体中文) built-in. Switch languages anytime from the top corner.</li>
</ul>

<h4>🕹️ Controls & Tips</h4>
<p>
  <strong>Mouse / Touch:</strong> Click or tap to read memorials, weigh the risks, and stamp your imperial decree.<br>
  <em>Tip: Don't ignore your Sanity—ruthless greed without rest is the fastest path to the Asylum ending.</em>
</p>
